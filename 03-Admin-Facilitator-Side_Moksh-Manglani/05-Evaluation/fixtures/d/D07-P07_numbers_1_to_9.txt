Numbers 1 to 9 - Mango Treat

Today we count mangoes. A basket holds 7 mangoes. Count them one by one: one, two, three, four, five, six, seven. The last number we say tells us how many there are in all.

The number names are: 1 one, 2 two, 3 three, 4 four, 5 five, 6 six, 7 seven, 8 eight, 9 nine. We write each number with a digit. The digit 7 has a flat top and a slanting line.

Meena has 4 mangoes. Her brother gives her 2 more. Now she has 6 mangoes. Counting on from 4 we say: five, six. Two more than four is six.

Comparing numbers: 5 is more than 3 because when we count, five comes after three. 3 is less than 5. If two baskets hold 8 and 6 mangoes, the basket with 8 has more mangoes.

Before and after: the number just before 6 is 5 and the number just after 6 is 7. Counting backwards from 9 we say: nine, eight, seven, six, five, four, three, two, one.

Activity: draw 9 mangoes on a paper plate. Colour the first 5 yellow and the rest green. How many are green? (4)
