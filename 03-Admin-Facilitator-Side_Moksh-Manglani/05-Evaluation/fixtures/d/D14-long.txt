Part 60: Angles and Shape Properties

An angle is formed where two lines meet. A right angle measures 90 degrees and looks like the corner of a book. An acute angle is smaller than 90 degrees. An obtuse angle is bigger than 90 degrees but smaller than 180 degrees. A straight angle measures 180 degrees and looks like a straight line.

The three angles inside any triangle always add up to 180 degrees. The four angles inside any quadrilateral add up to 360 degrees.

A square has 4 equal sides and 4 right angles. A rectangle has 4 right angles and opposite sides equal. A rhombus has 4 equal sides but its angles are not always right angles. An equilateral triangle has 3 equal sides and 3 equal angles of 60 degrees.
