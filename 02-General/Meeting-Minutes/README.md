# Meeting Minutes

[`SOFTENG-700A-Meeting-Minutes.md`](SOFTENG-700A-Meeting-Minutes.md) holds the minutes of all 19 meetings between 13 April and 14 October 2026, with a contents table at the top. Supervisor meetings were weekly through the year, with team-only meetings in between.

## Decisions worth knowing about

These are the meetings where the direction of the project changed. They are the quickest way to understand why the project ended up where it did.

| Date | Decision | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 11 May | Pivot from analysing student behaviour to supporting less experienced facilitators. The comparison with homeschooling in New Zealand showed that the difference was **delivery** | Defined the project |
| 12 May | Work split: Moksh took system architecture and requirements; May took facilitator research and the AI and learner-analysis side | Created the two parts |
| 20 to 25 May | Formal interviews planned, with ethics approval; build an MVP while waiting | Led to the ethics application (see `03-.../07-Ethics`) |
| 28 July | After the conference: argue explicitly for non-teachers running lessons, evaluate against criteria, and benchmark against existing systems | Shaped Chapters 3 and 6 of both reports |
| 5 August | Describe the concept as **training** facilitators, not only supporting them | Became the walkthrough and approval steps |
| 15 September | Replace the formal interview study with informal feedback chats, written up as design input | See `03-.../02-Informal-Feedback` |
| 7 October | Poster focuses on the technical work; future work is integrating the two systems for personalised learning | See `05-Future-Work-and-Handover` |
